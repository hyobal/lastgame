HOW RADAR WORKS

Momentum: Latest traded price compared with the opening price of the selected number of 1-minute candles.

Volume pace: Current 1-minute quote-volume run rate compared with the last 20 completed 1-minute candles.

Signal: Both price movement and volume pace must pass the selected thresholds.

Signals are volatility detections, not buy or sell recommendations. Scanner coverage depends on available exchange history and connectivity.
